# Dear Body Product Listing Image Strategy

Version 1.4. Shopify is the master sales channel. The system is designed for a responsive Shopify product gallery, collection grid, cart, checkout, and homepage product placements. The standard product gallery contains 12 images per SKU.

## Objective

Create a consistent product-listing image system that makes every Dear Body product:

- Immediately recognizable and easy to inspect.
- Premium, warm, vibrant, playful, and approachable.
- Clearly part of the Dear Body brand.
- Relevant to contemporary Filipino routines and the tropical environment.
- Accurate to the real product, packaging, scent information, and approved claims.

The listing should move from clarity to desire to confidence:

1. Show the exact product.
2. Communicate the scent and mood.
3. Show how it fits everyday life.
4. Answer practical buying questions.

## Non-Negotiable Product Fidelity

The supplied product photography is the source of truth.

- Keep the bottle shape, cap, pump, label, logo, typography, colors, proportions, and liquid level unchanged.
- Do not let image generation redraw the product label or create a new package variation.
- Composite the original product photograph into generated scenes whenever label accuracy matters.
- Generate backgrounds, props, shadows, surfaces, and lifestyle environments separately from the product.
- Add headlines, claims, dimensions, icons, and legal text after image generation using real typography.
- Never invent scent notes, benefits, ingredients, certifications, sizes, prices, bundles, or performance claims.
- Do not add fruit, flowers, spices, or materials as scent cues unless they are supported by the approved fragrance notes or creative brief.

## Recommended Listing Sequence

Use a focused 12-image gallery per SKU. Every frame must add a distinct buying reason or visual context; do not extend the gallery with near-duplicate angles, repeated poses, or decorative filler.

### 1. Primary Product Image

Purpose: immediate identification and storefront clarity.

- One product, front-facing, centered, and fully visible.
- Product occupies roughly 75-85% of the image height.
- Clean background with a natural grounding shadow.
- No decorative props covering the bottle.
- Use Dear Body soft cream `#f4e3cb` or a near-white neutral that gives the bottle clear separation.
- Keep this image free of headlines, badges, prices, and promotional copy because Shopify reuses the featured media across the storefront.

### 2. Brand Hero Image

Purpose: create desire and establish the Dear Body look.

- Place the product on a warm editorial surface or a simple color field.
- Choose burgundy `#5c0006`, rich red `#9a1106`, burnt orange `#d46601`, golden tan `#e9a250`, or soft cream `#f4e3cb` according to the scent personality.
- Use tactile shadows, restrained grain, fabric, paper, glass, or skin-like texture.
- Keep the composition simple and the label readable.
- Optional short headline: two to six words.

### 3. Scent Story

Purpose: translate fragrance information into a sensory image.

- Show only approved top, heart, and base notes.
- Use ingredients as tactile still-life elements around the product, not as visual clutter.
- Favor top-down or three-quarter editorial compositions inspired by the brand playbook.
- Keep one dominant scent idea per image.
- Add concise note labels only after the imagery is finalized.

### 4. Mood And Occasion

Purpose: help the buyer understand the personality of the scent.

- Connect the fragrance to an approved mood, time, or occasion.
- Examples: fresh daytime energy, confident office wear, warm date-night depth, relaxed weekend playfulness.
- Use Filipino lifestyle context where relevant and natural: tropical daylight, commute, getting ready, social moments, or personal rituals.
- Do not use generic European luxury imagery that conflicts with the brand's approachable positioning.

### 5. Key Benefits Or Verified Claims

Purpose: communicate the strongest reasons to buy.

- Use up to three verified claims.
- Keep each claim short and scannable.
- Use clean Helvetica-style supporting typography and simple icons.
- Use high contrast and generous spacing.
- Examples must come from approved product information, not assumptions.

### 6. Product Details And Craft

Purpose: prove quality and reduce uncertainty.

- Show close-ups of the cap, atomizer, label, bottle finish, liquid, or packaging details.
- Use real macro photographs whenever possible.
- Keep retouching realistic; do not remove intentional packaging texture.
- Use small callouts only for factual details.

### 7. Size, Scale, And How To Use

Purpose: answer practical shopping questions.

- Show exact volume and dimensions.
- Include a hand-held or vanity-scale reference only when the real dimensions are known.
- Show the correct application method and spray distance if approved.
- Keep diagrams simple and factual.

### 8. Collection, Variant, Or Closing Image

Purpose: support cross-selling and brand recall.

- Show related variants only when the exact products are available as references.
- Clearly label the selected SKU or bundle contents.
- Alternatively, end with a lifestyle image and the line `A SCENT JOURNEY`.
- Keep every bottle accurate and fully recognizable.

## Supplemental Lifestyle Image Bank

Create up to four additional lifestyle images for priority SKUs. Each scene must add a new buying context instead of repeating the same room, pose, or model.

### 9. Morning Getting-Ready Ritual

Purpose: make the fragrance feel easy to include in an everyday routine.

- Use believable tropical daylight in an attainable Filipino bedroom, vanity, or dressing area.
- Show the product clearly before or after use; do not hide the label in a hand.
- Keep grooming items generic and free of third-party logos.
- Match wardrobe accents to the product packaging without turning the scene into a costume.

### 10. Work, Commute, Or Day-Bag Moment

Purpose: connect the product to a practical weekday routine.

- Use a desk, tote, entryway, elevator lobby, or commute-preparation scene when appropriate.
- Keep the fragrance upright, protected, and realistically scaled.
- Avoid implying that a full-size glass bottle is travel-safe unless the brand approves that claim.
- Do not show workplace or transport branding that could create endorsement issues.

### 11. Social Or Out-Of-Home Moment

Purpose: show the mood of wearing the fragrance beyond the home.

- Use an approachable cafe, casual dinner, music, or pre-event setting that fits the approved occasion.
- Favor candid preparation and personal style over formal luxury posing.
- Use contemporary Filipino casting and settings when people are visible.
- Keep alcohol, nightlife, romance, or age-specific cues out unless they match the approved audience and campaign.

### 12. Personal Ritual Detail

Purpose: add a close, sensory human moment without losing product clarity.

- Show a natural hand reaching for the bottle, a cap-removal moment, or a vanity placement.
- Keep hands anatomically correct and the product at realistic scale.
- Do not generate an atomizer, cap interior, or spray mechanism that is not visible in the reference photography.
- Keep the scent name and logo unobstructed whenever the front label is shown.

## Standard Infographic System

Every 12-image gallery contains four infographics: frames 08, 09, 11, and 12. Generate or select the photographic base first, then add all typography, icons, rules, and callouts with real design software or structured code. Never rely on AI-generated text.

### Product Profile - Frame 08

Purpose: communicate the strongest verified product facts in a fast mobile scan.

- Use one clean product image plus a short set of verified facts.
- Suitable facts include volume, product code, line, bottle appearance, cap, liquid color, or package format.
- Do not infer fragrance notes, performance, occasion, or benefits.

### Ingredients And Care - Frame 09

Purpose: reproduce practical package information accurately.

- Transcribe ingredients, volume, product code, barcode, safety copy, and care wording only from the physical package or an owner-approved copy source.
- Do not add, omit, reorder, or promote ingredients into scent-note or benefit claims.
- Keep the full panel readable at mobile gallery size.

### Scent Description - Frame 11

Purpose: tell the buyer what the scent feels like in the owner's approved words.

- Use the exact owner-approved scent-description sentence and record its source in the QA report or copy ledger.
- Do not turn the sentence into a note pyramid unless official top, heart, and base notes are separately approved.
- Supporting imagery may echo the approved wording, but it may not introduce unverified ingredients, notes, benefits, performance, season, or occasion.

### Who It Fits - Frame 12

Purpose: help the buyer recognize the personal style or sensibility that may connect with the scent.

- Start with `For someone drawn to...` or an equivalent inclusive phrase.
- Derive the statement only from the approved scent description.
- Use no more than one short sentence and three concise style traits.
- Treat the copy as an editorial interpretation, not a promise or universal truth.
- Do not introduce age, gender, status, popularity, season, occasion, longevity, projection, or unverified note language.

## Standard Shopify Gallery: 12 Images

The default product-listing gallery is 12 images:

1. Primary product packshot with no copy.
2. Brand hero using the Dear Body palette and the SKU accent color.
3. Packaging or unboxing image.
4. Product material or construction detail.
5. Lifestyle image with a clear product interaction.
6. Second lifestyle image in a distinct context.
7. Third lifestyle or personal-style image when it adds a new use case.
8. Product profile infographic: volume, line, product code, and verified physical details.
9. Ingredients and use infographic: the exact printed ingredient list plus concise usage and care instructions.
10. Closing campaign hero, normally using `A SCENT JOURNEY`.
11. Scent description infographic: the exact owner-approved scent-description sentence.
12. `WHO IT FITS` infographic: one inclusive person-fit statement derived only from the approved description.

If a required fact is unavailable, replace that claim with another verified product or packaging fact. Do not fabricate content to fill a frame.

### Optional 17. Product Video

Purpose: bring the scent personality and product ritual to life.

- Use an 8-15 second loop with immediate product visibility.
- Show one sensory product tableau, one usage ritual, and one clean closing frame.
- Keep the label readable and the story understandable without sound.
- Use MP4 for the delivery file and provide a strong poster frame.
- Confirm that the selected Shopify theme supports product-gallery video before production.

## Shopify Technical Standard

- Master aspect ratio: `1:1` for all featured and gallery images unless the final theme test supports a deliberate alternative.
- Master image size: `2048 x 2048 px`, the Shopify-recommended square size for polished product display.
- Color profile: sRGB.
- Photography and composite exports: high-quality JPEG.
- Transparency, logos, and simple line graphics: PNG when required.
- Shopify product-image ceiling: up to `5000 x 5000 px` or `25 megapixels`, with each file below `20 MB`.
- Keep the same aspect ratio across each product's gallery and across collection featured images.
- Upload a strong source file; Shopify creates responsive sizes and serves an appropriate browser format through its CDN.
- Add concise, descriptive alt text to every image, ideally around 125 characters or less.
- Use descriptive filenames such as `dearbody-midnight-bloom-01-packshot.jpg`.
- Do not end filenames with Shopify transformation terms such as `thumb`, `small`, `medium`, `large`, or `grande`.
- Test the final gallery on mobile and desktop at actual storefront size before approving the SKU.

## Visual System

### Composition

- Use square as the Shopify master format.
- Keep critical content within the central 80% safe area for later 4:5 and vertical crops.
- Use one clear focal point and a stable product silhouette.
- Alternate centered packshots, three-quarter editorial scenes, close-ups, and top-down compositions to create rhythm.
- Do not place important text or product details near crop edges.

### Color

- Warm tones should dominate, but each image should include enough contrast to keep the bottle clear.
- Use one brand anchor color plus one scent-specific accent and one neutral.
- Use cream instead of stark white unless the selected theme or art direction requires a white alternate.
- Avoid cool blue-led scenes, neon colors, heavy gradients, and monochromatic red-on-red compositions that hide the product.
- Scent-specific colors may extend the palette when they support the actual product and remain secondary to Dear Body's brand anchors.

### Lighting And Texture

- Use warm editorial light, soft directional shadows, and realistic highlights on glass or plastic.
- Keep reflections physically believable and consistent with the scene.
- Use tactile materials such as fabric, paper, fruit, botanicals, glass, smoke, stone, or skin only when relevant to the scent story.
- Avoid sterile catalog lighting outside the primary packshot.
- Avoid excessive bloom, haze, liquid splashes, or fantasy effects that obscure packaging.

### Typography And Copy

- Use Cenzo Flare Bold for short campaign-style headings.
- Use Helvetica Now Display for notes, benefits, dimensions, and supporting copy.
- When the licensed Cenzo Flare or Helvetica Now files are unavailable in the production environment, use Helvetica Neue Bold for headings and Helvetica Neue for supporting copy, and flag the substitution before final brand approval.
- Use uppercase sparingly for confident brand moments.
- Limit most listing images to one headline plus three short supporting items.
- Never rely on AI-generated text inside the image.

### Infographic Layout And Accessibility

- Reserve a consistent text zone covering no more than 35-40% of the square canvas.
- Keep one headline, up to three supporting facts, and one clear visual hierarchy per infographic.
- Use Cenzo Flare Bold for short campaign headings and Helvetica Now Display or Helvetica for factual copy.
- At 2048 x 2048 pixels, use approximately 96-144 px for headings and 48-72 px for supporting copy, then verify at mobile gallery size.
- Keep all text inside the central 80% safe area and maintain high contrast against the background.
- Use package-matched colors for SKU-specific infographics while retaining at least one Dear Body brand anchor color.
- Use simple line icons only when they improve scanning; labels must remain understandable without icons.
- Do not place text over a busy label, reflective glass edge, face, hand, or important product detail.
- Export a text-free photographic master and a separate typeset final for every infographic.

### People And Lifestyle

- Cast contemporary Filipino consumers and use authentic, attainable settings.
- Show confident self-expression, getting-ready rituals, commutes, music, social moments, and personal spaces.
- Use bright daylight, saturated pop color, playful accessories, movement, low angles, and selective direct-flash energy for the light and fun Dear Body publication style.
- Treat warm, fun, and light as a mandatory emotional gate for every people-led frame. It is not a requirement to use the same warm colors or repeat one scene across scents.
- Build each still around an active, candid beat so it feels captured from a lively social video rather than staged as a static catalog portrait.
- Preserve cool or deep scent colors when appropriate, but balance them with inviting light, natural skin tones, open body language, and approachable behavior.
- Give every scent a distinct location, action, wardrobe story, prop language, camera treatment, and social energy. Changing only the clothing or background color is not sufficient.
- Reject cold, severe, brooding, intimidating, over-formal, or emotionally distant lifestyle scenes even when they look technically polished.
- For this product family, a black background behind the perfume name identifies the men's line; a white background identifies the women's line.
- A black-label men's product may use male models only in every lifestyle image.
- A white-label women's product may use female models only in every lifestyle image.
- Do not use cross-gender gifting, presentation, couple, or background casting in a gender-specific listing gallery.
- Let the label-color gender marker guide all visible people, wardrobe, pose, accessories, and lifestyle context.
- Keep skin texture natural and avoid over-retouching.
- When a product is held, match its real size and grip behavior.
- Do not let a model, prop, or hand cover the logo or scent name.

## Generation Workflow

### Phase 1: Product Intake

For each SKU, collect:

- High-resolution front product photo.
- Back, side, top, cap, atomizer, and box photos when available.
- Exact product name, variant, volume, and dimensions.
- Product-line gender marker: black perfume-name label for men or white perfume-name label for women.
- Approved fragrance notes, ingredients, benefits, claims, and usage instructions.
- Target customer, mood, occasion, and price positioning.
- Required image count, dimensions, and prohibited content.
- Shopify theme name and the product-gallery layout used by that theme.
- Approved logos and any required legal or distributor text.

### Mandatory Pre-Generation Gate

Complete and verify this checklist before generating the first image:

1. Confirm the exact SKU and separate it from any product with exchanged or mismatched packaging.
2. Confirm the perfume-name label background: black means men's line; white means women's line.
3. Lock casting before prompting: men only for black-label products and women only for white-label products.
4. Verify the product name, volume, concentration, ingredients, barcode, product code, packaging materials, and approved claims against the supplied product photos.
5. Mark unconfirmed fragrance notes, longevity, projection, occasion, and performance claims as prohibited.
6. Approve the exact 12-image gallery sequence and ensure every image has a distinct purpose.
7. Confirm the SKU color theme and the Dear Body anchor colors to be used.
8. Confirm that product references are sharp enough to preserve the bottle, cap, label, package, and liquid accurately.
9. Review every lifestyle prompt for the correct gender category before submitting it to image generation.
10. Inventory the model's visible accessories and props; do not ask the model to hold a duplicate of an item already being worn or carried.
11. Do not begin generation when the SKU, label color, gender category, or required product facts are unclear.

Preferred photography:

- At least 3000 pixels on the longest side.
- Sharp label and clean edges.
- Straight-on perspective for the primary photo.
- Even light with no clipped highlights.
- Neutral or removable background.
- No filters, beauty effects, screenshots, or messaging-app compression.

### Phase 2: Product Preparation

1. Check the source image for focus, distortion, glare, color cast, and missing label detail.
2. Create a clean product cutout with transparent background.
3. Preserve an untouched reference copy.
4. Correct only objective photography issues such as dust, temporary glare, or background contamination.
5. Record the real bottle dimensions and dominant packaging colors.

### Phase 3: Creative Mapping

1. Assign a scent mood and approved notes.
2. Select a dominant brand color and a scent-specific accent.
3. Choose props and textures that are supported by the scent information.
4. Define the lifestyle setting and whether a person is required.
5. Approve the image sequence before generating multiple variants.

### Phase 4: Image Generation And Compositing

1. Generate the environment without embedded copy.
2. Keep open negative space for later typography.
3. Composite the real product cutout into the scene.
4. Match perspective, contact shadow, reflections, color temperature, and depth of field.
5. Generate a small number of purposeful variants, not random stylistic alternatives.
6. Add typography, icons, and verified information after the visual is approved.

### Phase 5: Quality Control

Every final image must pass these checks:

- Product shape and proportions match the source.
- Logo, label, scent name, volume, and packaging text are correct.
- Cap, atomizer, bottle material, and liquid color are accurate.
- All visible notes, claims, dimensions, and instructions are approved.
- Props support the real scent story.
- Lighting, contact shadow, and reflections are believable.
- Product remains legible at mobile thumbnail size.
- Text has clear hierarchy and sufficient contrast.
- No extra fingers, distorted hands, duplicated props, malformed objects, or accidental text.
- No duplicated accessories, such as wearing one pair of sunglasses while holding another, unless the approved concept explicitly requires multiple products.
- No third-party trademarks or unlicensed recognizable packaging appear.
- Every visible model matches the product category: men only for black-label products and women only for white-label products.
- The image meets the Shopify theme's dimensions and content rules.

## Prompt Framework

Use this structure for environment generation:

```text
Create a premium but approachable product-listing environment for Dear Body Philippines.
Format: [aspect ratio and resolution].
Scene: [surface, background, setting, and camera angle].
Mood: [approved scent personality and occasion].
Palette: [one Dear Body anchor color], [scent accent], and [neutral].
Props: [approved scent-note ingredients or lifestyle objects].
Lighting: warm editorial light with realistic directional shadows.
Composition: reserve [position] for the supplied product, keep the central silhouette clear,
and leave clean negative space at [position] for typeset copy.
Style: vibrant, sensory, playful, polished, contemporary Filipino lifestyle.
Do not generate a bottle, logo, label, packaging text, headline, claim, or watermark.
Avoid cool clinical lighting, visual clutter, fantasy splashes, and unrelated luxury props.
```

For image editing with the real product reference:

```text
Keep the referenced Dear Body product completely unchanged: preserve its exact bottle shape,
cap, pump, label, logo, typography, colors, proportions, and volume markings. Integrate it into
the approved scene with realistic scale, perspective, contact shadow, reflections, and matching
light. Do not redraw, reinterpret, cover, or replace any part of the product or label. Do not add text.
```

## Production And Approval Plan

Start with one pilot SKU before scaling the system.

1. Confirm the platform specifications and product information.
2. Produce images 1, 2, and 3 for the pilot SKU.
3. Approve product fidelity, color direction, lighting, and scent-language treatment.
4. Complete the remaining listing sequence.
5. Lock the approved visual recipe for related variants.
6. Upload a test set to a hidden or draft Shopify product and inspect the gallery, collection card, cart, and mobile crops.
7. Lock the Shopify recipe, then scale it to the remaining SKUs.

## Default Deliverables

- Master canvas: 2048 x 2048 pixels, sRGB.
- Working file: layered when compositing or typesetting is required.
- Shopify output: high-quality JPG for photography and composites; PNG only when transparency or line-art fidelity is required.
- Transparent product cutout: PNG.
- Lifestyle expansion: up to four distinct scenes for priority SKUs.
- Final Shopify gallery: exactly 12 images unless the owner explicitly approves a shorter set.
- Infographics: four typeset modules plus their text-free photographic masters, covering product profile, ingredients/use, exact approved scent description, and person fit.
- Approved copy sheet: exact headline, fact, claim source, and approval status for every infographic line.
- Archive: original photo, untouched reference, cutout, generated environment, composite, and final export.
- File naming: `dearbody_[sku]_[image-number]_[purpose]_shopify_v01.ext`.
- Alt-text sheet: one concise description per uploaded image.

## Information Still Required

The following details must be confirmed before final production:

1. Shopify theme name and whether its product gallery uses square, portrait, or adaptive media.
2. Product category or categories and the number of SKUs.
3. Exact fragrance notes, benefits, claims, sizes, and dimensions for each product.
4. Whether lifestyle images may include models, and whether model assets already exist.
5. Whether listings should be English-only, Filipino-only, or bilingual.
6. Whether Dear Body cream is approved for the featured image or a pure-white version is also required.
7. Whether prices, promotions, badges, and bundle information belong inside secondary gallery images.
