## Brand Essence

DearBody Philippines brings London's rich fragrance culture to the Philippines through London-formulated scents curated for the Filipino lifestyle.

The brand position is premium but approachable: international fragrance craft, translated into vibrant, warm, playful, everyday Filipino self-expression.

## Core Idea

**Tagline:** A Scent Journey

Use this idea to frame campaigns around movement, memory, personality, confidence, and the emotional transport of fragrance.

## Mission

To bring carefully curated fragrances from London to the Philippines, creating scents that complement Filipino tastes, lifestyle, and the tropical environment.

## Vision

To bring London's fragrance culture into every Filipino's everyday life.

## Personality

- Warm
- Vibrant
- Confident
- Playful
- Approachable
- Sensory
- Premium without feeling distant

## Color Palette

Use warm hues as the dominant visual signal. The palette should feel rich, fragrant, sunlit, and expressive.

| Role | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep burgundy | `#5c0006` | Primary brand anchor, premium backgrounds, high-impact headings |
| Rich red | `#9a1106` | Headlines, buttons, active states, campaign emphasis |
| Burnt orange | `#d46601` | Energy, product accents, social highlights |
| Golden tan | `#e9a250` | Warm fields, secondary accents, lifestyle backgrounds |
| Soft cream | `#f4e3cb` | Light backgrounds, text on dark fields, breathing room |

Practical guidance:

- Lead with burgundy, red, orange, tan, and cream.
- Keep layouts warm-toned overall.
- Avoid cool blue, neon, icy gray, and overly clinical beauty palettes.
- Use cream for contrast rather than pure white when possible.
- Use dark burgundy for a premium fragrance mood; use orange/tan for playful Filipino lifestyle energy.

## Typography

| Role | Typeface | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display | Cenzo Flare Bold | Hero headings, campaign titles, large brand statements |
| Body / support | Helvetica Now Display | Subheads, descriptions, UI labels, captions |

Practical guidance:

- Use uppercase display type for strong brand moments.
- Keep headlines short and confident.
- Use Helvetica Now Display for clean readability under bold display headings.
- Keep text blocks concise; the brand should feel visual and sensory, not copy-heavy.

Fallbacks when brand fonts are unavailable:

- Display fallback: `Helvetica Neue Bold`, as specified in the product publication strategy. Cenzo Flare Bold remains the production display face; the fallback is not the official brand font.
- Body fallback: `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, or another clean grotesk sans.

## Logos

Primary logo:

- The `DEAR BODY` wordmark.
- Use for covers, headers, core brand placements, and formal applications.

Secondary logo:

- The circular monogram mark.
- Use for avatars, stamps, small placements, packaging seals, favicons, and social watermarks.

Logo usage:

- Give logos generous breathing room.
- Use cream/light marks on dark burgundy, red, or olive-toned backgrounds.
- Use dark marks on cream or light tan backgrounds.
- Do not place the logo over busy imagery unless contrast is protected.

## Visual Direction

Dear Body should look like warm fragrance culture meeting everyday Filipino lifestyle.

Use:

- Product-led photography with bottles clearly visible.
- Warm editorial lighting, tactile shadows, grain, smoke, paper, fabric, or skin-like texture.
- Lifestyle scenes that feel local, confident, and attainable.
- Vivid warm backgrounds and product color blocking.
- Simple compositions with one clear headline and a small supporting line.
- Social layouts that feel current, lively, and approachable.

Avoid:

- Cold, sterile, overly clinical beauty visuals.
- Generic luxury minimalism that feels too distant.
- Heavy cool gradients.
- Overcrowded copy.
- Product imagery that hides the bottle or scent identity.

### Lifestyle Mood: Warm, Fun, And Light

Every lifestyle image must feel warm, fun, and light. This describes the emotional tone, not one fixed palette.

- Use inviting daylight or warm editorial light, healthy skin tones, expressive movement, and open body language.
- Show an active everyday beat: getting ready, reaching, applying, moving, carrying, sharing, or enjoying a familiar social moment.
- Add one playful visual hook through color, styling, props, gesture, crop, or tactile texture while keeping the product immediately readable.
- Make the still feel like a frame from a lively social video rather than a static catalog pose.
- Keep the setting, action, wardrobe, props, and social story different for every scent. Shared warmth must not become duplicated lifestyle concepts.
- Cool, blue, turquoise, lilac, or deep scent palettes remain valid, but the human mood must stay inviting and approachable.

Reject cold or clinical people photography, heavy brooding darkness, intimidating or aloof luxury posing, mannequin-like stillness, and products pasted into unrelated actions. ## Content Applications

Social media:

- Keep the warm palette dominant.
- Mix product images with lifestyle shots and phone/social mockups.
- Use short, confident copy.
- Let one color or one product image carry each post.

Publication materials:

- Should be visually appealing, vibrant, approachable, and attention-grabbing.
- Use product-forward image grids, editorial crops, and warm color fields.
- Keep the layout clean enough that the product remains the hero.
- Use the shared 12-frame fragrance-gallery architecture so every scent includes product, lifestyle, practical information, an exact approved scent description, and a concise person-fit interpretation.
- Source scent-description copy from an owner-approved deck or copy sheet. Do not infer fragrance notes, performance, season, occasion, or benefits.
- Phrase person-fit copy inclusively as \`For someone drawn to...\`, using only qualities supported by the approved scent description.
- Keep all infographic copy readable at mobile size. On cream or beige fields use near-black or oxblood; reserve yellow, lilac, and orange copy for sufficiently dark backgrounds.

Video materials:

- Highlight authentic Filipino lifestyle moments.
- Show fragrances fitting naturally into daily life with confidence and style.
- Build for vertical, social-first viewing with a clear subject in the center of frame.
- Favor warm light, saturated but controlled color, tactile close-ups, and quick, loop-friendly movement.
- Use playful product tableaux, such as a product surrounded by fruit or other sensory ingredients.
- Mix polished beauty rituals with relaxed lifestyle scenes: getting ready, applying a product, commuting, listening to music, or moving through a familiar personal space.
- Use top-down compositions and tightly framed details to make ordinary moments feel graphic and editorial.
- Keep the product or ritual immediately readable even when the styling is expressive.
- Treat the reference clips as motion-language inspiration; adapt the casting, setting, and product behavior to DearBody and contemporary Filipino life.

## Working Rule For Future Project Assets

When creating anything for Dear Body, start with this checklist:

1. Does it feel warm-toned and fragrance-led?
2. Is the product or scent mood immediately clear?
3. Does the layout balance premium craft with approachability?
4. Are the brand colors, display/body font roles, and logo usage respected?
5. Is the copy short, confident, and sensory?
6. Does every lifestyle image feel warm, fun, light, active, and distinct to its scent?
