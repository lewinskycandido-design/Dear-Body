# Men and Women collection banners — PASS

Four new lifestyle assets: Men light and evening, Women light and evening. Built-in ImageGen created the two base images, then used each exact local base as the referenced target for a lighting edit. Original PNGs remain in originals/. No legacy photography is reused. Prompts, tool output paths and edit-reference paths are in collection-banners-provenance.json.

| Asset | Result | Inspection |
|---|---|---|
| sj-banner-men-light | PASS | One adult Filipino man; cream linen, sunlit coastal terrace, inviting expression, plausible hand/parapet contact; clear left heading space. |
| sj-banner-men-dark | PASS | Same model, framing, clothes, pose and terrace; warm evening coastal light; skin and linen remain visible. |
| sj-banner-women-light | PASS | One adult Filipina; warm-red clothing and natural walking movement in a botanical city courtyard; coherent anatomy and architecture; clear left heading space. |
| sj-banner-women-dark | PASS | Same model, framing, clothes, motion and courtyard; warm golden evening illumination; skin and foliage remain readable. |

Each original and all four optimized exports were visually reviewed. No products, fragrance bottles, logos, text or watermarks appear. No other visible people or faces appear. Canva palette (#F4E3CB, #5C0006, #91100D, #A2490B, #EAAA3D) informed the art direction, with natural green plants and neutral materials. These are separate from the earlier homepage campaign. No arrows. The dark versions remain warm and welcoming rather than gloomy.

Native outputs: light 1916×821 and evening 1915×821 PNG (approximately 2.33:1, tool-native closest to requested wide format). Website exports: light 1800×771 and evening 1800×772 progressive JPEG, quality 87, explicit sRGB, no content crop or photographic color alteration during export. The theme can overlay accessible live HTML headings in the quiet left 45%; use a suitable UI scrim if needed for contrast, especially over the sunset. Suggested desktop object-position: center; mobile object-position: 72% center to retain the model if cropped.

All four derivatives have distinct SHA-256 hashes recorded in collection-banners-web.sha256. No theme files were modified.
