# DearBody — Pop Editorial Banners (v9)

Current campaign refresh based on the owner's supplied DearBody publication-materials reference. Scarlet, cobalt, lemon and green accents, tactile knitwear and denim, direct sunlight and playful gestures carry the reference's mood into distinct Filipino everyday settings. The scenes and people are newly generated; the reference composition is not copied.

## Delivered scope

22 campaign photographs: Light/Dark homepage heroes, Women/Men collection gateways, Women/Men collection headers, Scent Finder, Our Story and footer banners, plus six product header photographs shared by both visual modes. Shop All reuses the Women's header. The homepage and general campaign scenes remain product-free. Each product header shows one adult person and its scent's bottle.

All images were generated or revised with the **built-in ImageGen tool**. The exact submitted prompts and repair prompts are in [prompts](prompts/); source references, generator paths, review notes and hashes are in [provenance](provenance/). Original PNGs are in [originals](originals/); installed JPEG exports are in [final-web](final-web/). The JPEG export uses sRGB at native resolution, without cropping, enlargement or retouching. Responsive CSS performs the displayed crop.

Website typography stays live and editable: Cenzo Flare Bold headings and Helvetica Now Display Light supporting copy. Banner headlines are not baked into these photographs. Physical bottle labels retain the product's packaging lettering. The banner-only v9 refresh preserved original transparent logos and the then-current 72 product gallery images; its previous-gallery snapshot remains a historical record.

The later gallery import uses the owner's selected `output/product-listing/GALLERY_REVIEW_2026-09-24.html` for the six active scents only, with source folder `rtulle-and-satin` mapped to live handle `rtulle-satin`. `../reviewed-product-galleries/` records unchanged source PNGs, 1200px website JPEGs and 160px thumbnails. All 22 v9 banners, original logos, favicons, live fonts, gallery order and autoplay are preserved. Current gallery verification is recorded in [Review gallery import QA](../qa/REVIEW-GALLERY-IMPORT-QA.md).

## Layout and verification

Header and footer banners span the viewport and share `clamp(540px, 43vw, 650px)` height on desktop, `600px` on mobile. Category gateways keep their portrait layout. Mobile text overlays the image. Dark product headers retain the same burgundy gradient as other page headers.

Current verification is recorded in [v9 banner QA](../qa/V9-BANNER-REFRESH-QA.md). Native-image review is recorded in [root visual review](provenance/root-visual-review.json) and the individual/aggregate provenance records. Final installed bytes are locked by [asset-lock.sha256](asset-lock.sha256).

The theme ZIP is **DearBody-Theme-Update-Pop-Editorial-Banners.zip**. This is a local theme build; no Shopify settings, products, media or published theme were changed. Earlier campaign folders remain historical archives.
