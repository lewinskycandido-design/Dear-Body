## Final Filipino adult lifestyle campaign

ASSET: Final merchant-approved Filipino adult lifestyle campaign, with complementary women's and men's page variants if both are used.

PURPOSE: Show fragrance as part of approachable everyday Filipino self-expression, alongside the theme's product-led fallback scenes.

PRODUCT REFERENCE: Select one of the six launch scents per frame and use its original bottle and matching canister photographs above. Mojito Metallique or Charme Envoûtant can be used for continuity with existing page concepts. Existing generated human scenes are direction references only, not proof of final approval or product accuracy.

MODEL: Clearly adult Filipino model or models in a relaxed everyday moment. Plan any real photography with properly authorized final usage. Do not imply celebrity endorsement or testimonial.

COMPOSITION: One clear everyday action such as getting ready, adjusting an accessory, reaching for a bottle before leaving home, or arriving at a cafe. Hold or place the authentic bottle naturally at its real scale, with believable hands and a visible label. Give different frames distinct actions and settings. Keep a complete safe subject area for mobile or supply a separate portrait frame.

BACKGROUND: Familiar warm home or cafe setting; cream and burgundy with restrained red/orange/gold accents. Avoid generic black-and-gold luxury staging.

LIGHT: Inviting daylight or soft warm editorial light, natural skin tones, readable product detail.

MOOD: Warm, fun, light, confident, active, approachable and premium.

NEGATIVE SPACE: Reserve a calm area for optional live text. Keep faces, hands and products clear of text and controls. No captions, logos or marketing claims baked into the photograph.

DESKTOP RATIO: 16:9 campaign image, or the final section's chosen landscape ratio.

MOBILE RATIO: Separate 4:5 portrait or verified safe portrait composition retaining face, hand and full bottle.

STRICT PRODUCT LOCK: Preserve the selected real product's geometry, original cap, label, liquid, typography and packaging print. Do not invent a new DearBody bottle or resize it unnaturally in a hand. Avoid showing unverified use, ingredients, efficacy, duration, concentration or endorsements. Inspect anatomy and product contact as well as exact printed identity before final approval.

## Licensed fonts — separate production asset requirement

**Official DearBody font file required.** No official Cenzo Flare Bold or Helvetica Now Display webfont files are available in the workspace. Obtain licensed web-embeddable files for Cenzo Flare Bold (display headings) and Helvetica Now Display (UI/body). The merchant can later configure the supported font URLs in Theme settings after uploading the licensed files.

Helvetica Neue / Arial is a documented development fallback, not a claim to be the official typeface. The Canva font specimen confirms names; it does not supply font binaries or establish an embedding licence. Font work is not an image-generation task.
