## At-home art direction

Arrange the six fragrances as a cared-for personal collection on a wooden dresser, console, or vanity in a welcoming home. Use warm natural light, cream surroundings, wood grain, and restrained burgundy or red fabric details. The scene should feel lived-in, attainable, and editorial, with appealing, vibrant color rather than sterile display lighting.

Keep bottles identifiable and their labels unobstructed. Use a natural grouping with enough depth to feel like a home display, while maintaining realistic bottle scale, contact shadows, transparent glass, liquid transmission, and reflections. Avoid excessive props or a single oversized scent dominating the group. Recompose for mobile so all six remain visible and the collection stays the focal point.

## Copy and publication principles

- Make the image appealing, vibrant, approachable, and attention-grabbing while keeping the products central.
- Do not invent fragrance notes, performance, ingredients, occasion suitability, benefits, or other scent claims.
- Keep headline, supporting copy, and purchase controls as separate live web content; reserve clean space in the composition for legibility.
- Use **Cenzo Flare Bold** for display copy and **Helvetica Now Display** for supporting copy/UI. **Helvetica Neue Bold** is the approved fallback only; do not substitute an editorial serif.
- Keep any live copy short, readable on mobile, and strongly contrasted. Do not obscure bottle labels with web text.
