# DearBody Vaporwave — layout and identity

The user selected [Evah's homepage](https://www.evah.ph/) and [Elemental Collection](https://www.evah.ph/pages/elemental-collection) as layout references for DearBody. This fourth edition preserves that adapted shopping structure while giving it a warm Vaporwave interface.

## Shared shopping structure

- Home, Scent Finder, For Her, For Him, Our Story and Contact navigation, with a separate cart icon.
- Full-width lifestyle photographs and clear entry points to For Her and For Him.
- Collection introductions followed by alternating photograph-and-copy rows with a fragrance name, approved character, description, actual price and product-page action.
- The existing three-question scent quiz, story/contact pages and native Shopify product and cart forms.

The theme does not import Evah's source code, copy, product names, photographs or logos. DearBody does not gain an Elemental Collection or Discovery Set category. Collection rows use live `collection.products` and pagination. Priority fragrances use the approved DearBody descriptions; other products use merchant-provided content. Prices and destinations come from Shopify.

## Fourth visual treatment

Vaporwave here uses retro grids, sunset shapes, warm gradients and windowlike framed panels within DearBody's exact palette: **#5c0006**, **#9a1106**, **#d46601**, **#e9a250**, **#f4e3cb**. The existing photography retains real packaging colors. Decorative treatments remain separate from product artwork and brand marks.

The primary wordmark and secondary circular monogram remain in their established brand roles. Their provenance and replacement options are documented in `LOGO-SOURCE.md`. The original three designs remain independent: Memphis uses playful geometry, Glass uses translucent surfaces, and Bauhaus uses flat geometric divisions.

Scent descriptions originate in the workspace's `output/product-listing/SCENT_DESCRIPTION_SOURCE_LEDGER.md`. Short character labels interpret those approved descriptions, without inventing notes, concentration or performance claims. No new product photographs or scent claims are introduced by this UI edition.
