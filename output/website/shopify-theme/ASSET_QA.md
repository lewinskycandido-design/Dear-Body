# Memphis editorial image provenance — 24 September 2026

This layout revision reuses existing generated DearBody product and lifestyle assets; it does not generate new imagery. Original owner photographs remain untouched and are reference material, not bundled raw snapshots.

The six 900px product packshots retain the approved names, cylindrical transparent bottles, black caps and original ivory/black label families. Their source-image review was performed in the earlier storefront build. `IMAGE-PROMPTS.json` records generation prompts and product references. Small label monograms/glass details are generated representations, not pixel-identical originals.

Homepage imagery now uses the already-reviewed spray, bag and vanity images from the Glass/Bauhaus editions: `db-home-spray-v1.jpg`, `db-home-bag-v1.jpg`, `db-home-vanity-v1.jpg`. `HOMEPAGE-PROMPTS.json` and `HOMEPAGE-ASSET-QA.json` preserve that generation/review provenance. The home display uses `db-banner-story.jpg`.

For Her and For Him page banners reuse the matching three-scent lifestyle photos. Story uses the six-bottle console scene, and Contact uses the telephone/stationery scene. Banner photographs are displayed edge-to-edge with responsive cropping; a prior asset QA pass is not a claim that every crop preserves all labels. The collection editorial rows use each live Shopify product's featured image. The desktop spray hero uses a centered responsive crop to keep the headline and actions in view; its mobile image and the remaining homepage photos preserve their native proportions.

Primary and secondary DearBody logo reproductions are used in this edition; official masters remain replaceable in theme settings. See LOGO-SOURCE.md.

Fresh layout/commerce tests are documented in VALIDATION.md; historical Glass or earlier Memphis layout measurements do not validate this new arrangement.
