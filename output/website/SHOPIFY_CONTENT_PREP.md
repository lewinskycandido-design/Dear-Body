### Our Story (handle `our-story`)

Dear Body brings London's fragrance culture into everyday Filipino life. Our fragrances are London-formulated and curated for the way you live here — warm days, late nights, and every version of you in between.

We believe scent should feel personal, not precious. Warm, vibrant, and full of personality, each Dear Body fragrance is made to be worn easily and often: a small daily ritual that says something about who you are.

This is a scent journey. Start wherever you like.
