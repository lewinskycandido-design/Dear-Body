# Dear Body: first-launch ads strategy

Prepared 25 September 2026 · Owner confirmed no ads have run yet · Budget and margins not supplied

**Start with one sales campaign and three distinct creative ideas: find your scent, choose a two-item pair, and understand one fragrance.** Keep the bold, warm, faceless Filipino visual identity. Use the remaining scripts as a testing library, introducing them as you learn what produces delivered orders.

This is a recommended starting strategy, not a proven winner. I reviewed the 16-script pack, its visual overview, the current local website theme, and official Meta, Shopify and EasySell guidance. No Ads Manager performance data was available. The public website could not be retrieved in this review, so website findings describe the current local theme, not a verified live deployment. No ads have been launched or budgets changed.

## What to improve in the current ads

| Finding | Why it matters | Recommended change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strong warm colors, recognizable bottles, expressive hands and props | The brand already has a coherent visual identity | Keep the styling and exact packaging; vary the buying reason and action |
| All ten campaign scripts repeat the same seven-second shipping/COD opening | New viewers hear the offer before learning why this fragrance interests them | Retain your offer-first hierarchy, but test a shorter 5–6 second combined offer introduction with product visible immediately |
| Product introductions in the six original scent videos begin around 11 seconds | The scent's identity and appeal arrive late | Show the readable bottle from frame one and introduce its name and approved scent description sooner |
| Most shots are slow pushes or almost stationary holds | Visually different props can still produce very similar videos | Edit the first tests to 18–20 seconds, using three to five meaningful actions or cuts; compare later with longer edits |
| Personality ads explain traits but repeat a generic discovery line | Viewers still need help imagining the smell | Pair one approved personality idea with the exact approved scent description |
| Mission/vision is a 40-second explanation | It asks a new viewer to invest time before understanding the product | Use it as an organic brand introduction and a later warm-audience test |
| Existing overlays sit near the top and bottom of the portrait frame | Reels/Stories interface elements may cover important words or the logo | Check every finished ad in Meta's placement preview and reposition copy before launch; the storyboard preview is not an Ads Manager safe-zone test |
| AI scenes communicate mood but cannot prove the real cap or spray behavior | A generated demonstration is weak product evidence | Record a short, real hands-only cap/spray clip for product-detail tests; use an actual cap sound if presenting the click as a feature |

The shorter lengths are creative test hypotheses, not a platform rule. Meta recommends vertical Reels creative with audio and important messages inside the safe zone. Its guidance supports adapting assets for placements; it does not establish a guaranteed winning duration for Dear Body. [Meta Reels guidance](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads).

## The first campaign

| Setting | Starting recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Business outcome | Profitable delivered, paid orders |
| Campaign | One Sales campaign; website conversion location; Purchase optimization after an end-to-end order tracking check |
| Structure | One prospecting ad set with three ads if the budget can support them; start with two and rotate the third if the test allowance is very small |
| Audience | Adults 18+ in Philippine areas you can reliably deliver to; broad targeting initially, without many perfume-interest groups |
| Product-line rules | Keep women's white/cream labels with Filipina talent and men's black labels with Filipino male talent. Casting rules do not require six separate ad sets. Gifts and personal shopping may cross buyer genders |
| Placements | Begin with Advantage+ placements if available, with appropriate assets and previews for each enabled placement; inspect brand-altering creative enhancements before launch |
| Formats | Portrait 9:16 video for Reels/Stories; prepare a deliberate 4:5 feed version or a readable feed still. Do not assume an automatic crop preserves the bottle and copy |
| Landing pages | Send each ad to the page that fulfills its promise: finder, collection, or exact product |
| Later remarketing | Add a small warm-audience test only when you have enough visitors/engagers to deliver sensibly. A suggested later split is 80–90% prospecting and 10–20% warm audiences, not a required ratio |

This is my application of Meta's emphasis on simple accounts, varied creative, automation, sound data and validation. It is not a Meta-prescribed three-ad template. [Meta Blueprint: Performance 5](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/path/253157-performance-5). Choose the campaign objective around the business outcome rather than treating cheap views or clicks as proof of sales performance. [Meta Blueprint: campaign objectives](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/path/248574-campaign-objectives-ads-manager).

Do not split a small first budget across all 16 videos, six scent ad sets, separate gender groups, and several interest audiences at once. The six priority products remain available; the test simply gives a few messages a chance to collect useful evidence.

## Three launch ideas and the next test

| Priority | Idea and source assets | What it should answer | Destination and CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Launch A | Mojito personality, script 11 | Will a personality invitation help an undecided shopper explore? | `/pages/scent-finder` · FIND YOUR SCENT on the creative; choose an available native button such as Learn More if offered |
| Launch B | Two-item pair, script 08 collection | Does the free-shipping threshold encourage a larger order? | `/collections/all-fragrances` · Order Now |
| Launch C | Oud Mirage scent story, script 05 | Does a concrete scent description drive direct product interest? | `/products/oud-mirage` · Order Now |
| Next D | Magnetic cap, script 07, supplemented by real footage | Can a tangible product detail build confidence? | `/products/mojito-metallique` · Order Now |

Mojito and Oud are contrasting starting candidates from the approved range, not asserted bestsellers. This opening test compares whole creative concepts, including different scents and destinations. It cannot isolate the effect of a hook, scent or landing page. Once one concept shows promise, compare two hooks for the same scent and destination while keeping everything else consistent.

For A, measure whether finder visitors go on to products and orders. High quiz engagement with weak sales may indicate that the extra step is not helping. Later compare a finder destination against a direct collection page using the same creative and suitable CTA. Do not promise a scientifically exact personality match.

## Match the ad to the actual ordering journey

**Order Now ad → appropriate product/collection page → Add to order or quantity selection → Check out → COD delivery form → order confirmation.**

In the current local theme, adding two items does not change the website button to Order Now. Its selection label is **Add to order** and its proceeding label is **Check out**. The fallback native cart uses **Continue to checkout**. The revised last line in the ten Flow campaign scripts is:

> Pili ng two or more items para free shipping, tapos tap Order Now.

The theme displays free shipping for 2+ items and ₱80 shipping for one item. Confirm that the live EasySell form and final total apply those conditions in all advertised delivery areas before spending. The saved 24 September product snapshot lists ₱799 per bottle, but this is not a fresh live-price check; the proposed scripts therefore avoid price claims.

Open every destination on a mobile device from the actual ad preview. Confirm the expected scent, readable bottle, approved description, current price/stock, shipping condition, COD form and confirmation. The finder must reach useful product results and a clear way to order. Avoid sending every ad to the homepage.

## Tracking: check the COD path before spending

The theme explicitly hands orders to EasySell, and its app embed is enabled in the local configuration. This does not tell us which Meta dataset is connected or whether the current live events work.

Shopify supports Pixel and Conversions API data sharing and describes standard shopping events. A custom COD flow needs its own validation rather than assuming the normal checkout path covers it. Review the chosen sharing level and the store's customer disclosure alongside setup. [Shopify data sharing documentation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/analyze-marketing/meta-data-sharing).

EasySell documents a configurable Purchase/Lead conversion event, matching browser/server event IDs, and optional browser funnel events. Its CAPI does not send every upper-funnel event. It also warns about duplicate purchases when another app separately tracks the same EasySell order. Verify one coordinated tracking setup. [EasySell tracking FAQ](https://help.tyslo.com/en/article/meta-pixel-conversions-api-capi-faq-1sfm23z/), [EasySell duplicate-event guidance](https://help.tyslo.com/en/article/double-meta-purchase-events-potential-causes-1p7sjyf/).

Before launch, check one controlled test order through Events Manager and the store's order records:

1. A product visit is distinguishable from a selection, checkout/form opening and completed order submission.
2. Opening the COD form or adding a second item never counts as a completed Purchase.
3. One submitted order resolves to one deduplicated Purchase with the expected order value and PHP currency. Closing/reopening or refreshing confirmation must not create a new purchase.
4. The ad campaign, creative and landing page can be identified through consistent UTM parameters and the store's available attribution data. Keep personal information out of URLs.
5. The business records submitted, confirmed, cancelled, delivered and returned COD orders separately.

These are recommended checks, not tests completed in this review. No test order was submitted.

## Budget and what counts as success

Your daily budget, product costs, fulfillment costs and delivery success rate are still unknown. Choose a **total test amount you can afford to lose**, then divide it by the planned number of days. For illustration only, a ₱3,500 seven-day allowance averages ₱500 per day; this is not a required minimum, a sales forecast or an approved spend.

Set an allowable acquisition cost from actual unit economics:

**Allowable ad cost per submitted COD order = (delivery rate × contribution per delivered order) − ((1 − delivery rate) × average failed-order cost) − desired profit reserve per submitted order.**

Contribution means money collected minus product cost, packaging, shipping subsidy, COD/payment fees and other variable fulfillment costs, before advertising. Failed-order cost includes the actual expected handling and return costs; avoid counting costs twice. Compute single-item and 2+ item orders separately because their shipping economics differ. If the result is zero or negative, improve the offer economics before buying traffic.

Use these business measures to judge the launch:

| Measure | How to read it |
| --- | --- |
| Ad spend ÷ attributed submitted orders | Early acquisition cost; still includes COD failures |
| Ad spend ÷ attributed delivered, paid orders | More useful final acquisition cost; report only after delivery outcomes mature |
| Collected revenue, variable costs and ad spend | Determines contribution after advertising |
| Two-or-more-item share and average collected order value | Shows whether the shipping offer grows order value profitably |
| Clicks → landing visits → product/finder actions → submitted orders | Locates friction; these are diagnostic signals rather than the final success criterion |

Use the same order cohort and attribution convention when comparing spend and delivered results. Meta-reported purchases and ROAS can reflect submitted COD orders before payment is collected. Reconcile them with business outcomes rather than assuming they equal cash revenue. This is an accounting implication of COD, not a finding that your account currently misreports.

## First two weeks

| Period | Work and decision |
| --- | --- |
| Before launch | Verify mobile ordering, pricing/stock, offer conditions, placement previews and deduplicated order events. Set the total spend cap and allowable cost per order |
| Days 1–3 | Start the small creative set. Correct broken links, tracking or delivery immediately. Observe where visitors drop out; avoid frequent cosmetic changes |
| Days 4–7 | Compare spend, orders and order quality by concept. Give under-delivered ads no winner/loser label. Investigate ads consuming the pre-agreed test allowance without purchases before adding spend |
| Days 8–14 | Keep promising concepts, introduce one revised hook or one next concept, and reconcile mature delivery outcomes. Extend observation if COD delivery results are still pending |
| After the first reliable results | Increase budget gradually only when tracking, stock, fulfillment and contribution support it. Keep testing distinct messages; add remarketing only when the audience supports it |

An ad set distributes spend unevenly, so this first round is exploratory. Use a controlled A/B test when you need a stronger causal comparison, and avoid changing audience, scent, hook and landing page together in that test. There is no universal CTR or two-day rule that proves a fragrance ad is profitable.
