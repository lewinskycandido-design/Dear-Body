What to make

Ten Facebook feed stills: six priority-scent lifestyle ads and four homepage/story angles. The page identity set contains one square profile image and one wide cover. Each content concept below is a filming plan for a new video; the delivered images are still photographs, not finished videos.

Visual direction

Review the two owner-supplied publication and social-media reference images before every future generation or shoot. Use their warm, playful, bold editorial energy without reproducing the compositions. Burgundy, rich red, orange, golden tan and cream dominate; scent colors add accents. Use photographed fabric, skin, glass, chrome and glossy surfaces, with real directional shadows.

Casting and framing

Cast Filipino adults only. Women hold white/cream-label Mojito Metallique, Amber Oud Silk and Mistened Narcissus; men hold black-label Charme Envoûtant, Oud Mirage and Rtulle & Satin. Film hands, forearms, lap and torso only. Keep every face, chin, mouth, eye, reflected face and background portrait outside the frame. Check the full motion, including the performer leaning forward.

Offer and script order

Start with “FREE SHIPPING / ON 2+ ITEMS.” Follow with “CASH ON DELIVERY.” End with “ORDER NOW.” The two-item condition stays attached to free shipping. English on-screen copy is paired with conversational Taglish narration, preserving the meaning of the approved scent descriptions. Never reduce the opening to unconditional “free shipping.”

Typography and composition

Use actual Cenzo Flare Bold for display copy and Helvetica Now Display for support. Use cream offer cards and burgundy text, with compact graphics that leave the bottle and hands visible. Add typography during editing, not on photographed props. Keep the product name, cap, glass base and key gestures clear of text. Review the finished 4:5 still at 360 px wide as well as full size.

Capture workflow

Record a vertical master, keeping the meaningful action inside a central 4:5 crop. Lock exposure and white balance for each setup. Begin with a steady master, then capture details and movement. Use a tripod for labels and the cap demonstration. Record each action at least three times, with two seconds of stillness before and after. Capture a separate high-resolution still rather than relying on a motion-blurred video frame.

Product continuity

Match the actual bottle and reference: small 50 ml straight-sided cylinder, thick clear base, exact label and liquid, fitted glossy black cap. The black cap has a black lower edge; silver hardware belongs to the neck/atomizer. Support glass by its body. Keep labels dry, clean, front-facing and unobstructed. Spell MISTENED, RTULLE and CHARME ENVOÛTANT exactly.

Edit and sound

Use quick tactile opening actions, then slower label holds. Avoid frantic zooms, artificial liquid effects and transitions that distort packaging. Allow scent descriptions time to be read. Record the cap click, zip, ceramic tap and cloth rustle separately. Use licensed music and lower it beneath speech. Subtitles must remain legible over the photograph, with no words covering labels.

Export and handoff

Delivered feed images: 1080 × 1350 PNG. Profile: 1080 × 1080 PNG, with the logo inside a circular crop. Cover: 1640 × 720 PNG, with central offer copy checked in a narrower preview. Reels should be edited from the vertical footage, with text placement checked in the actual publishing preview. Select an appropriate collection or product destination for Order Now when setting up each post or ad.

Concept 01 / Mojito Metallique

Out the pouch, into the playlist

Make the bottle an immediately recognizable part of a colorful getting-ready moment, then prompt a two-item order.

Scene

Tight waist-level crop of an adult Filipina wearing a golden-yellow ribbed top and dark denim. A red canvas pouch sits across her lap; blue headphones touch the lower-right corner. The entire story happens in her hands. A coral wall and yellow fabric keep the frame warm and graphic.

Casting: One adult Filipino woman. White/cream label women's bottle. Hands, arms and torso only; no heads, faces, partial faces, reflected faces or background people.

Wardrobe and color

Golden-yellow ribbed cotton, dark blue denim, coral manicure and one amber bangle. Keep shoulders, neck, face and head outside every shot.

Props

Correct capped Mojito bottle; red canvas crossbody pouch; one blue headphone set. No fruit, cocktail, mint or lime props based on the scent name.

Lighting

Use direct side daylight and a white bounce near the label. Preserve golden liquid and natural skin. Flag the black cap so it has one controlled highlight.

Camera and framing

Shoot vertical from waist height, angled slightly downward. Use a normal or short telephoto view rather than a close wide-angle lens. Lock the hero crop with the whole bottle centered; no camera tilt above the chest.

Performance

Open the pouch, lift the bottle by its body and steady its base, then turn it only enough to show the label. A gentle headphone tap provides playful movement. Do not suspend the real bottle by its cap.

Audio

A licensed upbeat instrumental, a clean zip sound, cotton rustle and one headphone tap. Keep voiceover forward and clear.

Shot-by-shot script

00–04

Action: Start with the full bottle upright just above the open red pouch. The left hand steadies its base while the right hand opens the zip; label visible from frame one.

Camera: Locked tight crop, full bottle readable from the first frame.

Concept 02 / Amber Oud Silk

Your finishing detail

Introduce Amber Oud Silk through a personal getting-ready ritual with an expressive orange and burgundy palette.

Scene

An overhead view of a glossy tangerine chair seat. A burgundy beauty pouch is open, with a closed cream compact and one coral cosmetic tube inside. A Filipina's peach sleeve enters as her hand lifts the perfume. One cropped iced drink may sit well away from the product.

Casting: One adult Filipino woman. White/cream label women's bottle. Hands, arms and torso only; no heads, faces, partial faces, reflected faces or background people.

Wardrobe and color

Peach cotton sleeve, natural coral nails and one delicate plain chain ring. Warm orange and burgundy dominate; cream gives the label space.

Props

Correct capped Amber bottle, burgundy pouch, closed compact, coral hair ribbon, one cosmetic tube. All accessories unbranded and the compact closed to avoid a reflected face.

Lighting

Strong window or open-shade sunlight with a small bounce into the label. Keep pale-peach liquid light and transparent, not yellow or red.

Camera and framing

Performance

Reach, lift, settle. Use small natural wrist movements and let the ribbon supply secondary movement. Keep the bottle upright and fingers beneath its name.

Audio

A light licensed groove, soft pouch zip, compact closing sound and fabric movement. Avoid heavy cinematic perfume sound design.
