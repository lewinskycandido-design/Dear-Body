## Creative treatment

Bold warm orange, red, burgundy, yellow and cream; tactile photographed surfaces; compact cream offer graphics; no visible faces or partial faces. Generated Filipino adult hands/torso scenes follow the product-line mapping: white/cream labels for women, black labels for men. Bottle names and liquid colors follow the supplied product references.

Every ad and the cover lead with FREE SHIPPING ON 2+ ITEMS, then CASH ON DELIVERY, and include ORDER NOW. The avatar prioritizes the PH monogram.

The raster photography and tactile avatar were generated with the built-in image tool. Added text was typeset using the actual Cenzo Flare Bold and Helvetica Now Display fonts. The original supplied logo source remains unchanged; the avatar is a photographed badge rendition of its identity.
