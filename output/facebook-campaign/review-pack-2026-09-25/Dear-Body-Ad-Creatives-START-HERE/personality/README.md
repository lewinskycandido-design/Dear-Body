# Dear Body — Find Your Scent

Six additional personality-discovery creatives, one per priority scent.

Open gallery.html for the six finished images, download links, captions and expandable filming scripts.

- final/: six 1080 × 1350 PNG ads.
- ad-copy-and-scripts.md: captions, ad headlines, scenes, camera and lighting direction, and six 27-second reel scripts.

## Campaign message

Main creative message: personality. Each scent has its three approved personality traits.

CTA: FIND YOUR SCENT.
Supporting question: Which scent fits your personality?
Configured destination: /pages/scent-finder, verified in the local website theme.

Free shipping on 2+ items and cash on delivery remain supporting copy. The personality campaign replaces ORDER NOW with the discovery CTA.

## Visual and product rules

Warm bold editorial photography, distinct fresh scenes, no visible faces or partial faces, Filipino adult hands/torso. White/cream-label products are paired with women; black-label products with men. Actual Cenzo Flare Bold and Helvetica Now Display fonts are used for added typography.

The two owner visual references were reviewed before generation. The project instructions now record the discovery CTA for this follow-up set. Previous campaign outputs are preserved.

Built-in image generation was used for photography and two precise label corrections. Rendered text was added with editable HTML/CSS. Photo bases and rendering sources remain in this project folder.
