## Production Notes

- The exact source-photo bottle is composited into frames where label fidelity matters.
- Scene environments and selected clean packaging cutouts were created with the built-in image-generation tool.
- All final typography was added after generation using Helvetica Neue as the available Helvetica-style fallback.
- The supplied publication image was used as a loose mood reference, not copied as a layout.
- No fragrance notes, concentration, longevity, projection, occasion, or performance claims appear in the gallery.
