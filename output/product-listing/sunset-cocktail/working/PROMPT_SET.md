# Sunset Cocktail Image-Generation Prompt Set

Mode: built-in image generation.

The final gallery uses generated scene environments and selected clean product cutouts, followed by deterministic source-photo compositing and typography. The supplied publication image informed color energy, playful styling, and product-forward framing only; its layout was not recreated.

## Global Scene Guardrails

- Square luxury-commercial composition with strong central-safe-area framing.
- Leave the designated product area empty so the exact photographed product can be composited later.
- Use realistic editorial light, tactile surfaces, and physically believable shadows.
- No text, logos, labels, perfume bottles, brand marks, watermarks, or unrelated products in generated environments.
- Dear Body palette: burgundy, rich red, burnt orange, golden tan, peach-coral, cream, and black, with selective bright blue and green accents.

## Final Scene Prompts

1. **Primary packshot:** Soft cream seamless studio sweep, subtle peach warmth, centered empty space for one upright bottle, restrained contact-shadow zone, clean ecommerce finish.
2. **Brand hero:** Burgundy textured editorial set with warm peach spotlight, tactile paper or fabric feel, left-side copy space and right-side space for bottle plus canister.
3. **Packaging reveal:** Cream, peach, and deep-red geometric set with warm daylight and an empty central stage sized for an open cylindrical package set.
4. **Cap detail:** Top-down burgundy and peach paper composition with a thin golden ring, left-side copy area, large right-side circular focal area.
5. **Red vehicle lifestyle:** Contemporary Filipino man in burgundy sunglasses leaning from a glossy red vintage car in tropical daylight; clean red door area reserved for the bottle.
6. **Tropical sky lifestyle:** Upward male hand against vivid blue tropical sky, one green sunglass pair and one red pouch, open palm and clear central space for the bottle.
7. **Streetwear lifestyle:** Cropped male styling in a yellow mesh top and black denim against coral and burgundy, with a burgundy plinth reserved for the product.
8. **Product profile:** Cream and peach editorial infographic environment with an architectural burgundy curve, left-side information space, right-side pedestal for one bottle.
9. **Ingredients and care:** Deep burgundy textured left field and curved cream right panel, left pedestal for bottle, large right text area, restrained peach divider.
10. **Closing hero:** Burgundy campaign stage with peach sunset disc, warm directional shadows, central foreground for bottle and canister, clear title space above.
