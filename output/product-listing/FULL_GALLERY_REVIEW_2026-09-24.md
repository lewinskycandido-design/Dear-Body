## Scope and validation

The audit covers product geometry and physical labels, canister construction, nozzle/mist alignment, cap material and embossing, fixed frame roles, full-person active lifestyle, the single-face budget, realistic hands and contexts, exact source-ledger scent descriptions, derived person-fit copy, legibility and final export integrity. Each final directory contains exactly 12 unique numbered 2048×2048 PNGs. Passing frames were checksum-locked before replacement; the baseline was independently compared after all repairs.

ImageGen photo outputs were normalized from their native generated size to 2048×2048 sRGB. Information layouts were typeset deterministically at 2048×2048. Visual review is separate from the technical gallery guard; guard alone cannot verify appearance. Rejected originals and failed attempts were deleted at the user’s request. Raw source references, accepted images and prompts are retained.
